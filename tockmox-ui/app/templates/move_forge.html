Move the site to my own forge

The site's whole history (every branch and tag) moves to an empty repository you own elsewhere, and one change repoints Argo CD at it. That change waits for an approver other than you, like every change. Once it is merged, Finish the move archives the built-in repository: read-only, nothing deleted.

Cancel
